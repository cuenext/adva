## 1. Purpose
The Company may give the Contributor information, media, footage, visual identities, plans, quotes, client briefs, schedules, production files, supplier details or system access to evaluate, quote, perform or discuss creative, media, marketing, staffing or production work (the “Purpose”). This Agreement governs confidential access only; no project, fee, employment, visa or assignment is promised.

## 2. Confidential information
“Confidential Information” includes non-public material disclosed orally, visually, in writing, through the ADVA platform or in media files; client identities where not public; strategy and campaign plans; NDA-protected job briefs; internal pricing; contracts; financial details; accounts and credentials; proposals; deliverables; photo/video footage; raw files; patient or other personal information; unpublished work; and reasonable copies, excerpts and analyses.

It does **not** include information the Contributor can document was: (a) public without a breach, (b) independently developed without using Confidential Information, (c) lawfully obtained from a third party without a duty of confidence, or (d) already known without restriction before disclosure. A lawful disclosure obligation must be handled under clause 6.

## 3. Use and handling obligations
The Contributor will: (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and only for an explicitly authorized assignment; (b) not disclose it to third parties without prior written authorization; (c) protect it with reasonable security, including strong passwords, MFA where available, updated devices and restricted access; (d) not send raw footage, customer data or credentials to personal AI tools, storage services, public portfolios or messaging groups without express permission; (e) not share ADVA logins, invite substitutes or subcontract work without approval; (f) promptly notify ADVA of suspected loss, theft, unauthorized disclosure or compromise; and (g) follow agreed client, site, event and brand requirements.

## 4. Media, people and permissions
No client names, logos, patient images, personal data, unreleased work, behind-the-scenes footage or campaign deliverables may be posted on personal social accounts, shared publicly or added to a portfolio without **prior documented authorization** from ADVA and, where necessary, the relevant rights holder or person filmed.

The Contributor must observe applicable consent, advertising, privacy and location rules. Healthcare and patient-identifiable media require particularly careful handling and appropriate clinic permissions. ADVA may ask for deletion or removal of unauthorized material.

## 5. Ownership, licensing and deliverables
This NDA does not itself determine copyright ownership of new creative work, equipment or pre-existing materials. Every engagement must have a separate written scope or project agreement covering fees, deliverables, revision rights, ownership or assignment of economic rights where legally permitted, licensing, moral rights and use of third-party assets.

Neither Party may imply ownership of copyrighted material it does not lawfully own.

## 6. Required legal disclosures
If legally required to disclose Confidential Information, the Contributor must, where legally permitted, provide prompt notice and disclose only the legally required minimum. Neither Party is required to breach applicable law.

## 7. Privacy and security
The Contributor may process personal information only on documented instructions, for the approved Purpose and for the agreed duration; must use appropriately secured devices and storage; and must inform ADVA promptly about any suspected data incident. ADVA will confirm lawful grounds, notices, retention and any cross-border transfer requirements before assigning personal-data processing tasks.

## 8. Return or deletion
On ADVA’s request or at the end of an assignment, the Contributor must return or securely delete non-public materials, copies and credentials within **[timeframe to agree]**, subject to any legally required retention. They must confirm deletion when requested and may not keep client files merely for their portfolio.

## 9. Duration and survival
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for **[duration to be approved]**. Confidentiality duties for material that remains non-public survive for **[survival period]**, with any trade-secret protection and legally mandated obligations considered separately by counsel. No unreasonable perpetual blanket term should be assumed.

## 10. Remedies
The Parties acknowledge unauthorized disclosure may cause harm. Remedies, compensation, injunctive relief and costs must be governed by applicable law and the final lawyer-reviewed terms, not unilaterally invented penalties in the website.

## 11. Independent contractor status
Signing, joining the ADVA platform, browsing a job or requesting enrollment does not create an employment relationship or authorize work in the UAE. The applicable engagement agreement, labour permissions, visa status, insurance and invoicing requirements must be checked for each assignment as appropriate.
